CSM SHEET SCANNER REMOTE — v0.4 REFERENCE ASSETS

OFFICIAL WORKFLOW
1. Generate and print official MRS forms only through the School CSM Control Center.
2. Do not print a blank template image directly. A form becomes official only when the Control Center inserts its registered Code 128 control number and submits it directly to a connected physical printer.
3. Open the Browser Survey Form on the phone and tap the scan icon.
4. Sign in with a Scanner Operator account registered in the Control Center.
5. Photograph the complete accomplished A4 form with all four corner markers visible.
6. Review the barcode, date boxes, shaded responses, warnings, and registry match.
7. Correct uncertain fields and finalize only after operator verification.

SERVER-HOSTED SCANNER
The phone scanner is hosted by the Control Center at:
http://<laptop-ip>:<port>/scanner

No standalone scanner package, permanent scanner key, or printable MRS PDF is required on the phone.

BUNDLED v0.4 ASSETS
The mrs_v0.4 folder contains English, Filipino, and Waray-Waray template images, preview images, language-specific coordinate maps, and the combined Control Center coordinate map used by the recognition engine. These are internal development/reference assets. They are not official pre-numbered forms.

CURRENT LANGUAGE SCOPE
English, Filipino, and Waray-Waray v0.4 printing and recognition are enabled. Each language uses a distinct four-marker set so the Control Center can identify the form language automatically.
